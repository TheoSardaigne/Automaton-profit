## 6. FAQ anglaise

**Do you find the competitors for me?** These packages compare companies you name and link. If you need competitor discovery, message me first; it requires a separate scope and is not automatically included.

**What if prices are not public?** I record “not publicly available” and the relevant source. I do not contact sales teams or estimate private quotes.

**Do you test the products?** No. This is desk research based on public pages. Features are reported as vendor claims, not tested performance.

**What do I receive?** An English HTML report and editable Markdown text, with a comparison table, summary, sources, access dates and limitations. PDF, Excel and Google Sheets are not included in these packages.

**What does the revision include?** One round of factual corrections or clarification within the original scope. New competitors, extra plans or a changed research question require a separately agreed scope.

**Do you use AI?** AI may assist extraction and drafting. A human checks the sources, price context and final report. If your project prohibits AI assistance, these packages are not a fit; please tell me before ordering.

**Can you recommend the best product?** I can explain trade-offs against your stated criteria. I cannot guarantee performance, return on investment or suitability without product testing.

**Can I see a sample?** Yes. My scheduling software comparison is an independent portfolio sample, not work for a client or an endorsement by the companies compared.

## 7. Informations client obligatoires, en anglais

1. What business decision should this comparison support? Give a short description; no confidential data is needed.
2. Name and link the 3 competitors (5 for Premium). Specify the product and public plans you want compared within the package limits.
3. Which country, currency and billing period matter to you? If a vendor does not publish these clearly, I will mark them unknown.
4. List your priority feature criteria (6 for Basic, 10 for Standard/Premium). If fewer are supplied, I will confirm a bounded set with you.
5. Confirm: public sources only, English HTML and Markdown delivery, no product testing, sales outreach or private data access.
6. Are there restrictions on AI-assisted research or drafting? If AI is prohibited, do not order this service; message me to confirm the mismatch first.
7. Is your deadline compatible with the package delivery time? Describe any special requirement before ordering; no rush delivery is included.
