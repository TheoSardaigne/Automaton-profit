## What a small business can use from this comparison

For a solo user, first qualify the free-plan limits against the required number of calendars and booking types. Team coordination needs require a different comparison: the entry paid plans above do not promise identical functionality. Round-robin routing, branded domains and delegation should become explicit evaluation criteria before requesting a final quote.

The displayed amounts occupy a narrow numerical range, but they are **not a normalized price ranking**. Annual commitment is explicit for two pages, while SavvyCal's text capture leaves the selected billing mode ambiguous. A dollar symbol alone does not establish a currency code. Do not calculate a purchasing decision or annual total until the vendor confirms currency, checkout amount and tax treatment. This sample deliberately preserves those unknowns rather than filling them with assumptions.

## Sources and limitations

All three official pricing pages above were consulted on **6 October 2026** through read-only web research. SavvyCal was also retrieved successfully with Aurum's public GET tool. Calendly and Cal.com exceeded that tool's 524,288-byte response limit and were verified separately by the operator through Codex's web reader. The final table is therefore a **reviewed joint deliverable**, not an autonomous Aurum output.

Prices can change or vary by location and selector state. No checkout, account creation, sales contact, performance test or paid subscription was performed. Feature presence is based on vendor statements; reliability, usability and commercial outcomes are not verified. Screenshots of toggle state, tax inclusion and definitive currency codes remain unavailable. A buyer seeking a purchasing recommendation should validate these points before committing.
